A single hero tree, pixel art, seen from a low top-down two-and-a-half-D isometric camera exactly like the reference island plate. Warm mid-brown bark with a soft sand-lit top edge and an umber shadow side, no white or cream stripe on the trunk; small pointed leaves in olive, moss and sap green; a crisp dark outline around the silhouette.
