What is being proposed — the one layout decision to judge

The time axis rotates: time now runs left → right, and depth indents downward. In the 360px panel time owned the abundant axis (vertical) and depth plus lanes shared the scarce one (360px of width). The bottom panel inverts which axis is abundant — it is roughly 1280 × 320, dragging between 160 and the map frame — so a plain 90° reading of “lanes side by side” would simply move the same crowding onto the vertical.

Rotating instead spends the new room on the axis that needed it, and the arithmetic is not close. Run the SHIPPED axis over the richest bundled trace at its shipped vertical config and it wants 608px of height; the bottom panel at its 320px default leaves the picture about 154px, so a vertical spine would scroll roughly four screens inside a strip two marks tall. Run the same algorithm along the horizontal instead and the whole 21-hour walk is 620 axis px, stretched to fill the ~1,100px the panel gives it — the entire trace, at once, with no scrolling in either direction.

Depth and lanes take the vertical in exchange. Depth is free: the deepest resolved descent in any of this machine's 346 traces is one level, so depth costs one row. Lanes are the real cost, and the worst case is not small — the widest packing anywhere is ten concurrent columns. That case is bundled rather than asserted: pick library-cli-0c7bf8 from the list and judge eleven rows in a 154px strip for yourself. It is the layout's stress test, and it is the strongest argument against the rotation as well as the clearest picture of what it costs.

ADR-0354 D3 permits this (“lanes may sit side by side, depth may take real horizontal room, and the axis may stretch” are permissions, not directions), but it is the reading worth confirming, because every other choice below follows from it. If the rotation is wrong, say so first — the rest is cheap to redraw.

What the rotation buys, concretely

  • The bar stops competing with the picture. It becomes a thin vertical track on the right instead of a full-width horizontal element above, so the traversal dominates the first glance by area rather than by good intentions.
  • Offers and descents stop colliding. Branches PRINTED fan upward from the spine; branches TAKEN — depth, then lanes — hang below it. In the narrow panel both competed for the same sideways room.
  • Folds get honest labels. A constant stub is now vertical, so its duration is written down the stub; a 16-hour gap and a 6-minute gap still occupy identical pixels.
  • The picture re-flows rather than rescales. Row spacing is computed from the rows the data actually needs, so a single-column trace looks like a single column at any dock height. Try the 160px preset.

Two behaviours worth agreeing to explicitly

  • At the dock's 160px minimum the CHROME yields, not the picture. The prose blurb and the honest-gap line fold away — both are still reachable in the drill-down and stated in these notes — and at the very bottom of the drag the legend goes with them. The alternative was letting the traversal shrink toward nothing, which fails the clause that says it must dominate the first glance. This is the bottom panel's analogue of the retired PANEL_MIN=360 rule, asked of height instead of width.
  • A trace longer than the panel PANS; it is never squeezed to fit. clever-mestorf-1041a3 is a nine-day walk with 41 folds and needs about 2,000 axis px, so the plot scrolls sideways and the playhead pans itself into view during playback. Compressing it to fit would destroy the density weighting that makes a burst readable.

What did not move — the signed grammar, verbatim

Layout was reopened; semantics were not (ADR-0354 D3). Each of these is drawn here exactly as signed:

  • One playhead occupancy bar, reading context resident in the window at the playhead, with the portion of the fill past 500k red and no marker, tick, or danger arc for the threshold.
  • Plain node marks, no per-node gauge — identity and read strength only, never a per-visit token readout.
  • Solid full-payload edges, grey dotted front-matter edges.
  • A magnifying glass for search — the only non-circular mark.
  • Branching carried by animation: an abandoned branch simply stops advancing. No revisit loop-back is drawn anywhere.
  • Explicit-only forks with a raw offered N, observable M of N — never a percentage (ADR-0312 D6).
  • No depth ever inferred from order, time, or the node graph. A trace with no resolved parent links renders as a single column, and two of the five bundled here do exactly that.

Honest gaps — what the real traces could not show, and why nothing was invented

The red never appears on real data

No local trace crosses the 500k threshold. The highest occupancy this machine has ever recorded is 430,616 tokens (recursing-neumann-3a74d7, bundled here) — so its bar runs long and stays entirely green, which is the correct drawing. Following the precedent the signed README set for the same situation, the rule is shown in the legend as an example fill that marks nothing, and it is exercised directly through the same render path by the “exercise the 500k rule” control above, which is labelled synthetic and is not a reading from any trace. No reading was fabricated to make the red show.

No lane can name its model, and no lane anywhere has a duration

PR #1272 added optional model / runtime fields to spawn_handoff. Counted across every trace on this machine: 0 of 115 spawn events carry model, and 4 of 115 carry runtime — all four in relaxed-snyder-c2f5c9, all reading sdk-leaf. That trace is bundled precisely so the difference is visible: its chips read red-builder · model not recorded while the drill-down names a runtime that WAS recorded. Nothing is ever defaulted.

Duration is starker. All 115 paired lanes span 0 or 1 millisecond — 100 at zero, 15 at one — and the maximum anywhere is one millisecond. These are --real build-leaf slices recorded retrospectively at completion, so the parent observed no interval at all. Each is therefore drawn as a point glyph rather than a bar: a band of any width would read as a duration nobody recorded. The rows are still laid out for bands, because a lane that ever does carry a span must have somewhere to draw it.

One defect this reference found, which the build increment inherits

A lane's band must be decided from its timestamps, not from the pixels its two ends map to. library-cli-0c7bf8 holds a lane whose handoff and return are one millisecond apart but fall either side of a folded idle span, so the axis places them 32px apart. Anything that measures the band in pixels draws that millisecond as a 32px bar — a duration produced by the fold rather than by the trace. This reference decides from the timestamps and draws it as a point. The same trap is reachable from yAt in the shipped renderer, so it is worth carrying into traversal-panel-wide-reflow as a red-green case rather than rediscovering.

Depth is real but shallow, and offers are almost never followed

Depth resolves to one level at most, anywhere on this machine, on 55 and 36 visits in two of the five bundled traces — so the picture indents by one row and no further. Across the bundled offers, the followed count is 0: followed_edge has a producer but is barely exercised, and ADR-0260 D4 makes under-reporting the accepted failure mode. A fan of not-followed rays is a correct drawing of a sparse signal, not a renderer bug.

Where the numbers came from

Every derived figure was produced by the shipped libraries, run over the real JSONL traces under ~/.storytree/traces, so this reference and the panel cannot describe the same trace differently: the axis by buildTraversalTimeScale, depth by computeTraversalDepth, lanes by buildTraversalLanes, the occupancy series by buildOccupancySeries, and every offer outcome by the server's own computeDecisionPoints. Nothing here re-classifies an offer or re-resolves a parent link.

The vertical-versus-horizontal figures above are the same function run twice over the same instants: TRAVERSAL_TIME_DEFAULTS gives 608 / 1,549 / 1,054 px for three of the bundled traces, and the wide config gives 620 / 2,001 / 1,220 px.

The axis config is the one layout choice: pxPerEvent 8, maxActivePx 1000, minActiveRunPx 12, foldPx 22, idle threshold 5 minutes — the shipped algorithm with a width-appropriate budget rather than the vertical one. The session list is this machine's real index, ; the five with a bundled replay are marked, and the rest are listed exactly as ADR-0354 D2 requires — offered and explained, never silently dropped.
