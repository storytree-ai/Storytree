the grain octave, on the GPU's own clock

A FRAGMENT-BOUND A/B scene: one ground quad, one draw call, filling the frame, so the ground's fragment shader is the dominant term. The existing hardware-floor scene draws one call per plant and is measured draw-call bound — quadrupling its fragments moved its number 0%, so a shader A/B there cannot resolve anything.

The page does nothing on its own. harness/frame-cost-measure.mjs drives it, calling window.__stFrameCost(spec) once per sample, round-robin across every variant and zoom. Each sample times the identical batch twice — once by wall clock around gl.finish(), once by EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2 — so the two routes can be compared on the same work at the same moment.
