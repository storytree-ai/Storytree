the land frame floor — a ground layer, on the GPU's own clock

Three arms over the shipped map, built by the same shippedGroundBuild the canvas itself calls, so the control arm cannot quietly stop being the map:

flat — the ground as it draws today.
grass — plus layer 1 of the approved ground: zero extra triangles, 23 extra lattice-noise octaves per ground fragment.
grass-amplified — the same layer evaluated 8x over, geometry untouched. This is the sensitivity control: if the clock cannot see it move, the run is UNVERIFIED rather than cheap.

The page does nothing on its own. harness/land-floor-measure.mjs drives it, calling window.__stLandFloor(spec) once per sample, round-robin across the arms. Timing is EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2 and nothing else — the gl.finish() wall clock the older hardware-floor page reports was measured blind to an 8.7x change in real GPU work, so it is deliberately absent here.
