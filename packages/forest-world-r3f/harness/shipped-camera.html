The owner reads the map’s camera as too low. The island’s on-screen size in pixels and its width-to-height ratio (the one number that tells the two causes apart: five degrees moves it by sin 50° / sin 45°, about 8%; the footprint moves it by 1 / sin 20°, about 2.9×); how tall a grove pine stands on screen (an upright foreshortens by cos, so a higher camera costs the trees); the land’s share of the frame; the colour-family census and MICRO against the reference; and pixels moved by more than 20/255 against today (ADR-0490 D6 — here it says only how different the picture is, never which is better).
