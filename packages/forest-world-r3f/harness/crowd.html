Thirty-five islands — does the bought dressing still read as a forest?

PR #1693 dressed one island from the kit and it read as a pine forest. It attached a caveat to its own recommendation: “nobody has seen a crowd of these islands together, and that’s exactly the kind of thing that looks fine on one and turns to soup on four hundred.” This page is that measurement, at the crowd size that actually exists — the real forest’s 35.

Four arms, one variable: bare (the control), today (the wild composition), kit (dressed from dressing-kit.glb, merged per island) and kit-merged (the same props and the same pixels, merged across the whole forest — the draw-call remedy, measured rather than suggested). A driveable surface, not a gallery: crowd-measure.mjs reads the pictures, the two readings and the frame cost off window.
