How pale the wheat is — a stop-luma lift on the rebased ramps

The wheat's six stops are the green's ramps rebased onto the owner's mustard anchor with no free constant, and every rung of that ladder came out DARKER and duller than the flat yellow token — the recipe's ramps sit below the token they mix into. The anchor cannot fix that (a paler anchor goes peach). This page ladders the one lever that can: a single lift on all six rebased stops in linear space, ratio-preserving, so the hue the owner picked stays and only the field's brightness moves.
