The shipped map’s ground — flat, and standing on the land’s relief

Two arms on the island the studio actually ships (164 parcels), differing in exactly one thing: which relief field src/cell-ground-geometry.ts is handed. flat is the identity field, which is the shipped buffer byte for byte as it drew before 2026-08-30; relief is landRelief, which <ForestWorldCanvas> now passes unconditionally. Same parcels, same status colours, same framing, same light — the light and the framing are imported from the shipped files rather than transcribed, so this is the product’s land and not a plausible one.

The relief adds no triangles, no draw call and no attribute channel: the vertices this ground already emitted simply stand where the land is and face the way it turns. shipped-land-measure.mjs reads the pictures and the GPU clock off window.landRunner.
