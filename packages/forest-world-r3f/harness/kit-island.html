A whole island, dressed out of the bought kit — beside the island as it is dressed today

Three arms on one island: bare (the control), today (the wild composition — procedural canopy lathes, plants, flower markers) and kit (the same ground, dressed from dressing-kit.glb). Every prop in the kit arm carries a signal read off the island’s own capabilities: a pine per proven contract, standing dead wood where a capability is unhealthy, undergrowth where one is not built yet, rocks for drift, logs for retired work, blooms for signed criteria. A driveable surface, not a gallery — kit-island-measure.mjs reads the payload, the frame cost and the pictures off window.
