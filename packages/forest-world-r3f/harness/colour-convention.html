Is every bought texture on this surface sampled in the land’s own colour convention?

This renderer is deliberately not colour-managed, so a glTF base-colour map decoded the ordinary way renders about 3.5× dark — and looks like a moody art direction rather than a bug. For every material each committed asset carries, this draws three swatches in one context: the asset’s own map, a flat swatch of that map’s mean, and a flat swatch of that map’s mean after linearising. The last two are what the frame would look like under each convention, measured rather than modelled, so the verdict is read off a control in the same run. Driven by colour-convention-measure.mjs.
