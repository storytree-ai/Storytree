# Five pieces of 0.2's forest are left out

- **Front cover of:** stories/forest.md
- **Full record:** ADR-0635 in storytree 0.2's decision log, storytree-ai/storytree02 (`pnpm storytree library artifact adr-0635`)

The forest leaves out five pieces of 0.2's forest, by the owner's decision: "cut all five".

They were the forest's proposed cuts, put to him one by one after he ruled that nothing that worked
in 0.2 is cut from 0.3 without his explicit decision (ADR-0633). He answered a table of the five.

- The 2D map. The 3D forest takes the clicks itself.
- The rig for measuring and choosing looks. The look is ported as it stands and judged by his eye,
  with a screenshot at each landing that changes it.
- The website mount. Hosting anything is out of the MVP, so it waits for 0.3's website.
- Build wisps and subagent tints. 0.3 has no builds or build roles; a claim is a marker with its
  agent and reason, and the agent's red and green show on the trees and in the drill-down.
- The story panel's pannable sub-map and session dock. The drill-down explains a story's parts with
  a small diagram, and who is on what shows on the forest and in the arc surface's overlay.

What each gives up was named and accepted: a flat, lighter view; checking the look by numbers; the
public site showing 0.3's forest during the MVP; a live on-island sign of red-then-green in
progress; and a pannable map of a big story's parts, with a per-story list of sessions. Any of them
can come back later as a new decision on this shelf.
